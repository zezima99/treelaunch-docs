Processing details

Purpose and operations. The customer selects source pages, destination and copy options. TreeLaunch reads identifiers, titles, hierarchy, space information and returned metadata; checks selections and title conflicts; and calls Confluence's copy API. Selected page content and optional attachments, labels and content properties are copied within the customer's Confluence site. The provider does not use that content for advertising, analytics, model training or unrelated purposes.

People and data. Data subjects can include users and any people mentioned in customer-selected content, including employees, business contacts and customers. Data may include names, account identifiers, authorship metadata, timestamps, page titles, communications and other personal information embedded in pages or selected attachments. The customer controls the content. No assertion is made that all customer content is free of sensitive or special-category data. Any such processing remains subject to the customer's lawful instructions and the applicable Atlassian service terms; the app supplies no special regulated-data certification.

Frequency. Processing occurs when an authorized user opens the app and requests a copy. TreeLaunch has no scheduled background collection or separate customer-content database.

Duration and deletion. Transient app processing lasts for the requested operation. Copied pages are customer-controlled Confluence content and remain until the customer deletes them under its own retention settings. Uninstalling the app does not delete those pages. The customer uses Confluence's access, export and deletion controls; provider assistance is limited to what TreeLaunch can actually access and control.

Support correspondence. Provider-managed contact details and correspondence are handled separately for answering support requests. The existing approved policy is deletion as soon as unnecessary and normally within 30 days of receipt unless law requires longer. Customers are instructed to send minimal, redacted diagnostics and no confidential page contents or credentials. This retention policy does not delete Confluence pages.

Hosting and subprocessors

The legal entity is identified in the current Atlassian Developer Terms and incorporated Forge DPA. Confluence is also the customer's separately contracted service.

Atlassian's processing locations and downstream providers are described in its subprocessor list, scoped to Forge and Confluence, and the Forge privacy FAQ. In-scope stored Confluence content follows the customer's supported residency settings. This is not a guarantee that every account record, support activity or transient processing operation stays in the EEA. Applicable transfers use the mechanisms in the relevant contracts. TreeLaunch does not claim its own Data Privacy Framework certification.

No separate app backend, analytics vendor, AI service or external app-content destination is configured. GitHub hosts documentation and source/deployment tooling, not the app's customer-content database.

Support correspondence is handled separately by the provider using STRATO GmbH email hosting, under the privacy notice. It does not route in-app Confluence content to STRATO.

Technical and organizational measures

  • Confluence calls run as the current user; platform authorization controls access. Backend code checks the active license before copying.
  • App scopes are restricted to the Confluence reads and page writes in the existing manifest. Users do not supply API tokens or passwords to TreeLaunch.
  • The code has no external app-content egress, separate persistent content store, advertising or analytics integration. It does not intentionally log page bodies, account details or full API responses.
  • Source restrictions are not copied. New pages inherit destination access; customers must check the destination before copying.
  • Interrupted jobs may leave partial copies. The interface reports partial results; customer cleanup can be necessary.
  • Atlassian provides platform infrastructure controls. TreeLaunch itself has no security certifications and no completed CAIQ Lite, as confirmed by the owner.

Mazen Eltawil is the responsible privacy and security contact at support@treelaunch.de. The support process uses minimal, redacted diagnostics; verifies customer requests; and reviews records at least weekly to meet the 30-day default retention policy. Incident response records discovery time and known scope, limits evidence to what is necessary, contains the issue, and provides customer notices and updates within the adopted DPA’s deadlines. New subprocessors are assessed and notified in accordance with the standard DPA.
