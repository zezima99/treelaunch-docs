TreeLaunch · Confluence Cloud documentation

Copy the pages you need.

TreeLaunch helps you select pages from a Confluence page tree and copy them beneath a destination page, in the same space or another space on the same site.

Start a copy

  1. Open the source page's action menu and choose TreeLaunch — selective clone.
  2. Select the pages to copy. Selecting or clearing a branch changes its descendants too.
  3. Enter the destination page URL or numeric page ID and optionally a title prefix or suffix.
  4. Choose whether to copy attachments, labels and content properties.
  5. Run preflight, resolve any title conflicts, then launch the clone. Keep the app open until it finishes.

What to expect

  • Selected pages retain their hierarchy. A page whose parent is excluded is placed under its closest selected ancestor, or directly under the destination.
  • Existing pages are not intentionally overwritten. Title checks run before copying and again immediately before each write.
  • Copies use your Confluence permissions and inherit destination access. Source restrictions are not copied.
  • Links and macros may continue to reference original resources. Review the copied content.
  • Operations run one page at a time. A failure can leave partial results; inspect the destination before retrying.
  • The current release supports trees of up to 300 items including the root and 50 levels. Larger trees must be divided into smaller branches.

Availability depends on Confluence access, service limits and an active subscription or trial where required. This website provides documentation; purchases and installation are handled through Atlassian.
