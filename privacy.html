Data processed by the app

TreeLaunch is a Confluence Cloud app hosted on Atlassian Forge. When you open it, it reads source and destination page identifiers, titles, hierarchy and space information so you can select pages, check title conflicts and copy them. Confluence API responses may also include page metadata such as author or owner identifiers, timestamps and version information. These responses are processed transiently; TreeLaunch does not build user profiles from them.

The copy operation processes selected page bodies and, if enabled, attachments, labels and content properties through Confluence's copy API. These materials may contain personal information supplied by your organization. You control the selection and copy options. TreeLaunch does not request page bodies to display the selection tree.

Atlassian supplies installation, signed-in user and license context. TreeLaunch uses the current user's permissions for Confluence requests and checks active license status before copying. It does not request a separate TreeLaunch account or payment credentials.

Where processing happens

The app uses Atlassian-hosted Forge functions and Confluence APIs. It has no external backend, separate app-owned content database, advertising SDK or external analytics integration. It has no permission configured to send app data to external services.

Source and copied content remain within the customer's Confluence site. Copy results returned to the interface contain the new page identifier. The app does not send page bodies to the provider's email address. Backend code does not intentionally log page contents, account details or full API responses. Atlassian operates the underlying services and may maintain operational logs and service records under its own privacy policy.

Roles and data residency

Mazen Eltawil acts as a processor for customer-directed Confluence content processing and as a controller for provider-managed support correspondence. In-app stored content remains in Confluence and follows the customer’s supported residency settings. Confluence pages, attachments and in-scope metadata follow Atlassian’s data-residency policy; account information, space keys and access-control configuration are subject to Atlassian’s exclusions. Separate support email is outside the app’s Confluence residency scope. Data residency is not a promise that every platform or support operation occurs only in the EEA.

Retention and your controls

TreeLaunch retains no separate content copy after a copy operation. Created pages remain ordinary Confluence content until the customer edits or deletes them. Uninstalling TreeLaunch does not delete copied pages. Ask your Confluence administrator about access, correction, export or deletion of that content and your organization's retention rules.

Source restrictions are not copied. New pages inherit access from their destination. Review destination access before copying. Interrupted operations can leave partial results that you may need to review or remove.

If you contact support

Mazen Eltawil processes the email address, message and any diagnostic details you choose to send solely to answer the request and resolve the reported problem. Send the minimum necessary information: a description, app version, approximate time and a redacted error message. Do not send passwords, API tokens, payment details or confidential page contents. Unnecessary details are deleted when identified.

Support communications are deleted as soon as no longer needed and, by default, within 30 days of receipt, unless a longer period is legally required. Where a legal obligation requires retention, only the necessary information is retained for that period. This is the provider's support-handling policy; it is not an automated deletion feature of the app.

The business support mailbox is hosted by STRATO GmbH. Email handling is covered by STRATO’s applicable services and privacy information. Support correspondence previously sent to the former Gmail address remains subject to the same minimization and retention policy; changing the contact address does not extend its retention. Support messages are not copied into a public issue tracker.
