Support & contact

Help with TreeLaunch.

TreeLaunch is provided by Mazen Eltawil.

For product help, privacy requests or a security concern, email mm.maha71@gmail.com. For a security concern, start the subject with “TreeLaunch security”.

What to include

  • A short description of the problem and steps to reproduce it.
  • Your TreeLaunch version and the approximate time the problem occurred.
  • A redacted error message or screenshot, if useful.

Do not send passwords, API tokens, payment details or confidential Confluence content. Use a small example with invented data wherever possible.

Email support is available without a published response-time or uptime guarantee. Marketplace billing and subscription controls are managed through Atlassian.

Before retrying a copy

Check the destination for pages already created, choose a unique prefix or suffix, and run preflight again. TreeLaunch copies pages sequentially, so an interrupted operation may leave partial results.

How support information is handled

We request only information needed to investigate your question. Support correspondence is deleted when no longer needed and, by default, within 30 days of receipt unless legally required longer. See the privacy notice for details.
